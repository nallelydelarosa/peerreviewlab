The premise

Research shouldn't live behind a paywall and a 30-page PDF.

We use science to make sense of real life, internet discourse, relationships, pop culture, news, and whatever everyone is arguing about this week.

We show our sources. We talk about limitations.

And sometimes the scientific conclusion is genuinely: we have no idea.

Peer Review Lab is an independent science and culture media project making research easier to understand, more entertaining, and more useful in everyday life.
